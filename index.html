GAME HINTS

How to Play

  1. A visitor appears at the window, claiming to be a resident. Only let them in if they really are.
  2. Passport (on the tray) — the identity they present. E to inspect.
  3. Journal (the notebook on the desk) — the truth about every resident.
  4. Belongings (on the tray) — a keychain, letter, book or receipt may belong to someone else.
  5. Phone (the desk phone) — call the number they gave and listen.
  6. Intercom (on the wall left of the window) — ask them questions and compare answers.
  7. Press the green button to let them in, the red button to deny. Wrong choice = game over.
  8. Survive all 7 nights. Each night is harder.

Shapeshifters forge the passport, so it always "matches". The truth hides in the details — an off-by-one apartment, a stranger's keychain, a story that doesn't line up with the journal.

Don't trust one tool alone: on later nights a shapeshifter can answer every intercom question cleanly while the lie sits in a belonging on the tray or on the phone line. Cross-reference everything.
